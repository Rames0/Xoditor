## Known limits and untested paths

- No automatic Word-compatible pagination/layout engine, DOCX import/export, equations, tracked changes or concurrent collaboration. Page settings and explicit page breaks do not provide those features.
- Word paste uses HTML supplied by the browser. Images not exposed as clipboard files, floating drawing objects and complex multilevel numbering can differ. Pasting representative documents from real Word is still required.
- Complex malformed/ragged tables and resizing merged tables are not supported comprehensively. The latter remains deliberately disabled.
- Formatting still partly uses legacy browser `execCommand` behavior. Passing Chromium/Firefox fixtures does not establish Safari or every browser-version compatibility.
- Composition events were simulated. Actual Nepali keyboard/IME installations need manual verification, as do touch interaction and assistive technologies.
- Printing checks validate the generated HTML and one PDF page-size fixture. They do not compare every existing letter's printed margins, fonts and footer position against Microsoft Word or an approved physical sample.
- The authenticated GWP save, MinIO image upload and backend PDF workflows were not exercised end-to-end. Editor/adapter save-load behavior was tested locally.

The results establish a tested improvement of the custom editor, **not a claim of universal zero errors or complete Microsoft Word feature parity**.
